Talk Spot Prompter

A teleprompter that follows the words you say.

    Writing a script

    • ===== Section ===== makes a section you can jump to.
    • A blank line starts a new take.
    • [Cues in brackets] show in yellow and are never waited on.
    • ★ marks a line you want to cut into a Short.
    • Write numbers the way you'll say them.

    Filming with it

    • Voice follows your words. Ramble all you like: it waits until you're back on the script.
    • Tap any line to jump back to it for a retake.
    • Auto scrolls at a steady speed. Manual moves only when you tell it.
    • Put the iPad right under the camera lens and keep the reading line near the top.

    Keyboard or clicker

    • Space start or stop listening, or play and pause
    • ↓ ↑ next or previous line
    • → ← or Page Down Page Up next or previous take
    • + − scroll speed, M mirror, Esc back to scripts
